Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Studio analytics is a personal tool used only by the owner of one YouTube channel, on their own computer.

  • What it accesses: read-only YouTube Analytics and YouTube Data API information for the owner's own channel (video IDs, publish times and daily view counts).
  • What it does with it: stores those numbers on the owner's computer to compare the channel's own videos. Nothing is uploaded, edited or deleted on YouTube.
  • Sharing: the data is not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.
  • Other people: the tool does not collect data about any other person.
  • Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the local files.

Studio analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
